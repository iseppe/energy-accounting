# Primary and Secondary Energy Dimensions

Autistic and AuDHD experiences frequently impact multiple Energy Dimensions at once (for example, a loud cafeteria is both Sensory and Social), so restricting an Entry to a single dimension loses important context. However, attributing an Entry's full Energy Value to every attached dimension double-counts energy in summary breakdowns, while splitting values fractionally across tags distorts the recorded intensity. Every Entry therefore requires exactly one Primary Dimension (which carries 100% of the Energy Value in proportional daily and range breakdowns) and zero or more Secondary Dimensions (used for contextual tagging, filtering, and secondary occurrence counts).
